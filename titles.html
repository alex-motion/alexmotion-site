Cinematic Titles

A selection of title sequences and motion typography designed for film and video.

    MEET UGLY

    Main title and end credits for a 48-hour punk rom-com. Drawn with ink and marker over 20+ iterations, then composited into the film. Best of Show – Richmond 48 Hour Film Project.

  • Video pending export assets/video/sympho.mp4

    Sympho

    A moody, type-driven title sequence inspired by Taxi Driver. Combines custom typography, motion, and in-camera footage.

  • Video pending export assets/video/jazz-hand.mp4

    JAZZ HAND

    End title design for a 48-hour film about music, marriage, and a mangled metacarpus. A flickering neon sign evokes moody jazz club signage, while the glowing red hand foreshadows the film’s dark twist.

  • Video pending export assets/video/elegy-for-the-wind.mp4

    Elegy for the wind

    Title design set to evening footage of bats in flight. Slow-paced, minimal typography drifts through the frame—off-center and nearly silent.

  • Video pending export assets/video/ubud-pop.mp4

    UBUD POP

    Informational kinetic typography composited into real-world footage. An exercise in rotoscoping, tracking, and type placement under challenging lighting and motion conditions.
